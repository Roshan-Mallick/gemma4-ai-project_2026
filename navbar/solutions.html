Solutions

One scanner.
Four ways it stands watch.

SafeHire runs the same OCR → entity extraction → cross-check → AI analysis pipeline behind every plan — tuned differently depending on who's holding the shield. Pick where you sit.

01 · For individuals

Know before you apply, not after you've lost your deposit.

Paste a job post, forward the recruiter's email, or drop a screenshot from LinkedIn. SafeHire checks the company, the domain, and the person on the other end of the chat — in the time it takes you to read this sentence.

  • Instant red flags. Upfront payment requests, fake equipment fees, and "urgent hire" pressure tactics get called out by name.
  • Recruiter lookup. Cross-checks the person's LinkedIn history against the company they claim to represent.
  • Plain-English report. A risk score plus the exact evidence behind it — no security jargon.

02 · For career services

Protect an entire graduating class from one shared inbox.

Career centers forward dozens of suspicious postings a week from students who don't yet know what a scam looks like. SafeHire screens submissions in bulk and flags patterns targeting your campus specifically.

  • Bulk screening queue. Students submit postings through a shared portal; staff review a ranked risk queue instead of one-off emails.
  • Campus alerts. When the same fake recruiter targets multiple students, everyone gets notified — not just the one who reported it.
  • Onboarding-ready reports. Exportable evidence packets for student conduct or local law-enforcement referrals.
Talk to campus solutions →

03 · For talent teams

Defend your employer brand from impersonators.

Scammers pose as your recruiters to run advance-fee schemes — then applicants blame your company. SafeHire monitors for impersonation and gives your team a fast way to verify "is this really us?"

  • Brand impersonation monitor. Tracks lookalike domains and cloned job posts using your company name.
  • Verified recruiter badge. A shareable link candidates can check to confirm a recruiter is genuinely on your team.
  • Applicant trust page. A public "report a fake posting" form that routes straight to your security team.
See HR protection plans →

04 · For platforms

Screen postings before they ever go live.

Job boards and hiring platforms can call the SafeHire API directly in their posting pipeline — catching scam listings pre-publish instead of relying on user reports after the damage is done.

  • REST API & webhooks. Score a posting at submission time and hold it for review above your risk threshold.
  • Bulk domain intelligence. WHOIS, SSL, and DNS checks run at platform scale, not one listing at a time.
  • Dashboard for trust & safety. Analytics on scam volume, source patterns, and takedown turnaround.
